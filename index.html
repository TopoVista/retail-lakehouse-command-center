Azure Databricks retail pipeline

Retail Lakehouse Command Center

A Databricks medallion architecture project for ingesting, refining, and publishing analytics-ready retail data across Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers.

Bronze
Raw ingestion
Auto Loader brings source retail data into Delta Lake.
Silver
Refinement
Customer, order, and product entities are cleaned and deduplicated.
Gold
Business marts
Fact and dimension tables support trend and revenue analysis.
